Architectural Philosophy & Living Quality

Exclusive single-unit residential engineering in Sector 08, Jolshiri Smart City

Signature Release

Bayt-Al Noor by MARS Structure Limited represents the pinnacle of private residential architecture in Jolshiri Smart City. Situated on a prime 5-katha corner plot along Road 501 in Sector 08, this landmark development is engineered specifically for discerning families who refuse to compromise on privacy, light, and safety.

Built on an exclusive single-unit per floor master layout, each homeowner enjoys uninterrupted 360-degree daylighting and natural cross-ventilation across all living suites and master bedrooms. Every structural drawing and column load-bearing matrix has undergone rigorous vetting by certified BUET structural engineering professors, guaranteeing robust resilience against high seismic activity.

Expansive double-glazed thermal windows, oversized shaded verandas, imported porcelain finishes, and smart home provisions make Bayt-Al Noor not just a residence, but an enduring generational asset with rapid capital appreciation.
